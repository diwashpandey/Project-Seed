# Project Seed

Welcome to Project Seed! Our platform is here to make connecting with your school community easier and more rewarding. Whether you're a student looking for friends, mentors, or job opportunities, or a person seeking talented individuals, Project Seed has you covered.

### Give **Rise**, take **Rise**, and grow together

## Technologies Used
- **Backend**: Django
- **APIs**: Django Rest Framework
- **Frontend**: React
- **Other**: Various libraries for enhanced functionality

## Description
Project Seed addresses common challenges faced by students when starting at a new school and provides a solution for recruiters seeking talent from specific educational institutions.

### Challenges
- Finding friends and mentors in a new school can be tough.
- It's hard to connect with classmates who share your interests.
- Understanding the school community can be challenging.
- Recruiters struggle to find the right candidates from specific schools.

### Solution
- **Student Networking**: Easily connect with peers and mentors within your school.
- **Interest Matching**: Find classmates who share your interests and hobbies.
- **Comprehensive Community Insights**: Gain a better understanding of your school's community.
- **Talent Discovery**: Recruiters can easily find talented individuals from specific schools.

## Ranking System
Project Seed's ranking system recognizes and rewards active members of the community, not just the most popular students.

### How It Works
- **Teacher Likes**: 5 points
- **Student Likes**: 2 points
- **Achievement Recognition**: 1 point

This system ensures that all members who actively contribute are acknowledged, fostering a professional environment where everyone's efforts are valued. It helps new students connect with engaged peers and provides recruiters with a comprehensive view of the community's active participants.
